# Tone

- **Status:** approved 2026-09-30
- **Sources:** `VISION.md` §Vision statement, §Client constraints, §Art direction, §Success
  criteria, §Risks
- **Read by:** every piece of story, dialogue, UI text, sound and art starts from it; a task cites it
  when it writes player-visible text or chooses how something feels.

**Muted, melancholic, eerie. For all ages.** The dungeon is very old, it has seen every Akeris
before, and it is sad about it. The game unsettles; it never shocks, mocks or celebrates loudly.

---

## 1. The voice in words

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences, plain words, a little archaic in the spirits' mouths | modern slang, jokes, winks at the player, memes |
| Say less than is known; leave the rest to the next run | explain the paradox outright before the third incursion (outside the demo) |
| The spirits are tired, patient and kind, and a little afraid | spirits that are cheerful guides or menacing villains |
| Akeris does not know who he is; he is determined, not heroic | boasts, catchphrases, a chosen-one speech |
| UI text is neutral and brief: *Continue*, *Locked* | exclamation marks, hype, capitals — except the combat callouts |
| Combat callouts are hand-lettered and loud: *COUNTER!* | callouts anywhere outside combat feedback |

Every player-visible line is a translator key with its English text, and has a Spanish text of the
same tone. Neither language is a literal translation of the other; both say the same thing in the
same register.

## 2. The look

`02-art-direction/ART_DIRECTION.md` governs how things look. What the tone asks of it:

- **Dark and refined, not crude.** Stick figures with round heads, drawn with care: proportions,
  weight and silhouette read as eerie, never childish (`VISION.md` §Risks).
- **Restraint in colour.** Graphite and ink on old paper; warm torchlight; the red eye streak (purple
  for the corrupted timeline); ice-blue and fire accents only where a shield or effect is ice or fire.
- **Death is ink.** Defeated enemies dissolve into ink smoke. No blood, no gore, no bodies left
  behind.
- **Stillness.** Nothing trembles; the only motion is real motion. A quiet room stays quiet.

## 3. The sound

`07-audio/` governs the sound. What the tone asks of it:

- One muted track per zone; menus and boss fights soft, never triumphant.
- Spirits speak a processed ancient language, never a readable real one.
- Impacts are dry and short; silence is allowed.

## 4. For all ages

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| fear, loss, loneliness, the end of the world as a threat | gore, dismemberment shown as flesh, blood |
| monsters, skeletons, wraiths, a corrupted self | cruelty dwelt on, torture, profanity |
| tearing a bat's wing or a boss's slab as drawn damage | anything that reads as injury to a person |
| Akeris's death, drawn without gore (the pose is `03-characters/akeris/`) | a gruesome death animation |
