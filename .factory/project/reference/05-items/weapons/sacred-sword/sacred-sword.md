## 1. What it is

The sword Akeris always carries without knowing what it is: dormant and weak in the demo. It
activates with the first gem, which is not in the demo (`VISION.md`); its gem holes are not drawn.

## 2. Look

As in his hands (`blade` `#e4e1d9`, 114 long). The icon lies at −45°, point up and right.

## 3. How he gets it

He always carries it, from the first second of every run; it cannot be lost, dropped or sold.

## 4. What it does

Melee only: the combo, the overhead strike, the aerial attack and the counter. Weapon levels 1–3 in
the demo (1→2: 1000 XP, 2→3: 3000 XP, `VISION.md`); **Each level raises the weapon's damage** (D-I8), by how much is the game's. Its
level does not change its drawing (`items.md` I11).

## 5. Icon

[`icon.png`](icon.png): the sword alone, transparent, 96 × 96 su.

## 6. Text

`item.sword.name`: Sacred Sword / Espada sagrada.

## 7. Never

- No gem holes in the demo; it never glows.
