## 1. What it is

The pair of hook blades, each on its own chain: the ranged weapon and the tool for rings, walls,
snatching and anchoring. Always carried, starting at a low level (`VISION.md`).

## 2. Look

As in his hands (`hookBlade` `#d2cdc2`, `leather` grips). The icon shows both blades crossed, hooks
out, joined at the pommels by a short chain, as they hang at his belt.

## 3–4. How he gets it, what it does

Always carried; never lost. Levels 1–3 in the demo with the weapons' costs (`VISION.md`); **Each level raises the weapon's damage** (D-I8); a level
does not change the drawing.

## 5. Text

`item.chainBlades.name`: Chain Blades / Espadas-gancho.

## B. The chain does not hold enemies still (operator, 2026-10-06)

The ranged weapon attacks at range as before; its hits never interrupt or push an enemy back; each enemy's chain reaction
at most once every 5 s; bosses hit four times in a row by the chain let out a sound blast that pushes Akeris away without
damage. Detail: [`hook-blades.md` §6](../../../03-characters/akeris/weapons/hook-blades.md).
