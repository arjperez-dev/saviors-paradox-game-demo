## 1. What it is

A heavy two-banded iron head on a wooden handle, carried on the shoulder: the slowest weapon, with the most knockback; its overhead slams the floor.

## 2. Look

As in his hands (`wood` and `iron`, `02-art-direction/palette/palette.md` §5). On its altar it floats
tilted, at 0.8 of its size; the icon lies at −45°.

## 3. How he gets it

On a stone altar, in any zone, by the run's seed; **once per run** (operator, D-I1). G takes it: it
goes to the first free slot and he draws it at once (I2). Lost on death.

## 4. What it does

The same inputs as every weapon: the combo (diagonal, rising, thrust), W + click overhead, the
aerial attack, and the counter with the sacred shield. Levels 1–3 in the demo (1→2: 1000 XP, 2→3:
3000 XP, `VISION.md`); **Each level raises the weapon's damage** (D-I8). Its damage, reach in world units and cooldowns are the game's; its feel is
in its animation file (I13).
