## 1. What it is

The shield Akeris always carries: unbreakable, the only one whose perfect block against a melee hit
opens the counter (G16). It stops physical attacks, not fire or ice.

## 2. How he gets it, what it does

Carried from the start of every run; never lost, never broken, so it has no uses, no broken icon and
no repair. It is upgraded with XP in the full inventory; its levels and costs are an open question
of `VISION.md`. A level does not change its drawing (I11).
