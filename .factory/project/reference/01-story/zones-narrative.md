# What each zone tells

- **Status:** approved 2026-09-30
- **Sources:** `VISION.md` §Content scope, §Bosses, §Abilities · `premise.md` §4 · `characters.md`
- **Read by:** whoever writes a zone's biome, its spirits' lines or its boss. The lines themselves
  are in `dialogue/zone-N.md`; how the zone looks is `04-zones/`.

Each zone adds one piece of the suspicion (`premise.md` §4). The pieces are spread so that a player who
dies early still hears something new every run, and one who reaches zone 5 has all the hints the
demo allows.

---

| Zone | What it was | What it tells | The hint it adds | The boss, in the story |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Suspended Sanctuary | the sanctuary where Akeris's people climbed to hear the clock. When the clock was used below, the stones forgot how to fall | where the clock was kept; that the dungeon rebuilds itself each time | the spirits know Akeris already; the sword has three hollows | the **Golem**, the sanctuary's keeper: it lifts whatever enters, because holding things up is all it was taught. | 2 · Echo Caverns | caves that keep every sound ever made in them | that Akeris's people went down one by one to bring the clock back, and none came up | the steps ahead may be Akeris's own, from before; one came down with a red light and the echo came back another colour | the **Giant Bat**, blind, living on the echoes; when it breaks, it scatters |
| 3 · Sunken Graveyard | the graves of everyone who came down for the clock. The gravedigger digs them before they are needed | that the dungeon does not let its dead stay still | every tombstone bears the same mark: three hollows in a row, like the sword's guard | the **Skeleton**, the oldest of the dead, who stands up once more after falling |
| 4 · Rotting Canopy | the roots of the world above, grown down into the dark and rotting as the days run out | how little time the world has left | someone waits at the bottom of everything, holding the clock, counting | the **Giant Spider**, weaving in the roots |
| 5 · Dragon Battlements | walls Akeris's people built, not to keep things out, but to keep something from coming up | that fifteen floors lie below, and nobody the spirits remember came back from the bottom the same | someone watches Akeris from the walls, walks the way he walks, and does not want him to go on | the **Dragon**, the last guard; it forgot why it guards, not how |

The outro pays off zone 5's hint: the one watching from the walls is Future Akeris, who stands right
behind Akeris in the Dragon room's fog (`cinematics/outro.md`; changed by the operator 2026-10-14). The intro sets up zone 4's: the one holding the clock has a purple streak
(`cinematics/intro.md`).

## Where the spirits stand

The story asks each zone's `layout.md` for two things; where exactly is the generator's choice:

1. **The lore spirit** stands off the main path, so finding it rewards exploring (`00-game/pillars.md`
   §4).
2. **The warning spirit** stands on the way to the boss room, before its door, so every player who
   reaches the boss has passed it.
