# T058 — Frame a box in pencil on paper

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Outcome:** `FlashKind` (an enum of `denied`/`done` with each's `Duration`), `InkFlash` (a value
  object: a kind and a serial), `FrameLook` (an enum of the five papers, each with its fill and
  hovered fill) and `FrameStyle` (a value object: seed, look, focused, hovered), all at
  `lib/core/ui/presentation/`; and `InkFrame`, a `StatefulWidget` at
  `lib/core/ui/presentation/ink_frame.dart`, sized in hu, that paints a box's paper and its pencil
  frame — thin at rest, thick with an inner line when focused, dashed when `FrameLook.dashed` — and
  flashes the thick frame 4 hu out, fading from full to nothing over its `FlashKind`'s duration, when
  given an `InkFlash` different from the last one. Its box, dashed-focus and flash strokes are
  generated once per size and seed, again only when either changes.
- **Decisions:** none the task left open — the task file's `Implementation` was followed for every
  signature in `Interfaces`.
- **Deviations:** `ink_frame.dart`'s single `_InkFramePainter` (as the task file's `Implementation`
  gave it) was split into `_InkFrameBoxPainter` (the paper and the frame or the dashes, behind its own
  `RepaintBoundary`) and `_InkFrameFlashPainter` (the flash alone, `repaint: flash`, outside that
  boundary), with the shared thick-frame-and-inner-line drawing pulled out to a top-level
  `_paintFocusedFrame`. The task's own `coding-standards.md` §11.4 ("a static painted part sits behind
  a `RepaintBoundary`") and `checklist.md` F4 require this: as given, the single painter repainted the
  static paper and frame every tick of a 350–400 ms flash, alongside the fading overlay — exactly what
  T057's `PaperSheet` (built from the same checklist item) already avoids. The `tester` found this gap
  against checklist F4 and it was closed before `verify-task`; no file outside `files` was touched by
  the fix (the split stayed inside `ink_frame.dart`).
- **Evidence:** `flash_kind.dart`, `ink_flash.dart`, `frame_look.dart` and `frame_style.dart` were put
  in verbatim from the task's `Implementation`; `ink_frame.dart` as described above. Tests were written
  test-first against each `Assertion`: layout size via `tester.getSize`; paint order and colour/width
  via `flutter_test`'s `paints` matcher, comparing stroke widths with
  `Float32List.fromList([w]).first` as the criterion requires, since `Paint.strokeWidth` round-trips
  through a single-precision buffer; the flash's fade verified at 175 ms (`Palette.ink` at alpha 0.5)
  and its end at +200 ms (no width-3.6 path); the three "does not flash" cases (built with a flash, the
  same flash given again, after a flash ends) each pumped one frame and asserted no width-3.6 path;
  the child's placement via `tester.getTopLeft`. One test (the flash-fade one) was confirmed to fail
  correctly when the fade's `1 - flash.value` was broken to `1`, then restored, before this registry
  entry was written. The `tester` subagent (started after the task's own tests and
  `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` were green) found all 15 acceptance criteria covered and reported one
  checklist gap (F4, above, closed) and one borderline note on checklist C7/P5 (`_paintFrame`'s
  if/else-if/else over `FrameLook` is not an exhaustive `switch`; left as is — `FrameLook` is explicitly
  "a closed list of data" and four of its five cases share one branch). `flutter test` (383 tests) and
  `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` are clean; `verify-task` passed.
