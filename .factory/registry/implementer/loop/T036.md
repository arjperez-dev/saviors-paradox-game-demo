# T036 — Step the interface systems after the world

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Outcome:** `StepClock` gains `clamp`, the frame clamped to the maximum step without touching
  the hitstop or the halts; `GameLoop` gains `interfaceSystems` and a `CommandDrain` (`drain`),
