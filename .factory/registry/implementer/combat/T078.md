# T078 — Fly the projectiles from a fixed pool

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Outcome:** `Flights`, the 64-slot object-pool aggregate root for projectiles in flight, with
  `Flight` (its part-private entity), `FlightTrack` and the `FlightEvent` family
  (`FlightStruck`/`FlightBlocked`/`FlightTurned`/`FlightCrashed`) added to `combat`'s domain. All 20
  of the task's named tests pass; `verify-task` passed first time.
- **Decisions:** where an assertion did not fix a body's position (`a turned flight flies level at
  1.6 times its speed`), I built a one-off `Defender` whose hurtbox sits on the flight's own
  position at the moment of the turn, since the assertion only constrains velocity and direction,
  not where any defender stands.
- **Deviations:** none against the task's `Implementation` as first written; it compiled and passed
  every criterion unchanged. The tester found three checklist items the first pass failed, all
  closed within the task's own files (rule 17), widening the `Implementation` as adaptations:
  - **A11** — `FlightTrack`'s constructor did not validate itself, unlike `Missile`'s identical
    `radius` field. Added `assert(radius > 0, 'a flight has some size')` in
    `flight_track.dart`.
  - **C4** — `Flights._struck` was 25 lines, over the 20-line function limit. Split the hitstop and
    affliction computation into `_struckHitstop` and `_struckAffliction`.
  - **C7** — `Flights.meet` chose between a block, a perfect block and a reflect with an `if`/`else`
    chain over `BlockVerdict` instead of an exhaustive `switch`. Extracted the guarded path into
    `_resolveGuarded`, which `switch`es exhaustively over `BlockVerdict` (no `default`); `_blocked`
    now takes the `Guard` and the `BlockVerdict` instead of a `ShieldId` and a bare `bool
    isPerfect`, to stay within the 4-parameter limit after the switch extraction.

  The tester's other observations (multiple simultaneously-flying flights, natural lifetime-expiry
  slot reuse, accumulated small `dt` steps near the 4 s boundary, the non-frozen/non-burning
  immunity branches of a reflected hit, the `towards == 0` tie-break in `Flight._turn`) are coverage
  notes, not failures of a named criterion or a checklist item; the task's test names are the
  planner's, so none were added.
- **Evidence:** files touched: `lib/features/combat/domain/flights.dart`, `flight.dart`,
  `flight_track.dart`, `flight_event.dart`, `test/features/combat/domain/flights_test.dart` — exactly
  the task's `files` list, no file outside it.
