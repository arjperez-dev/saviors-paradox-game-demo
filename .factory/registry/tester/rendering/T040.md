# T040 — Follow a target with the world camera

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Outcome:** All 14 acceptance criteria have a test under the `Test name` the task file gives, verbatim, each asserting the stated value: view height (750 at any resize), width-follows-aspect, the pre-resize 1920×1080 frame, the 60 % height placement, the 110-unit look-ahead asymptote in both facings, the 5/s and 3.5/s ease rates, confine-to-bounds on both the low and the high edge on both axes, the low-edge-wins narrow-zone rule (x only), confine re-placing at once, device-pixel snapping, rest after settling (`==`, not `closeTo`), determinism across two cameras, and the `toScreen`/`toWorld` round trip. `flutter test test/core/rendering/presentation/follow_camera_test.dart` passes 14/14; `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` and `dart format --output=none --set-exit-if-changed` on both files are clean.
- **Decisions:** none — the task file's acceptance criteria table has no `Manual check`; no manual test written.
- **Deviations:**
  - The look-ahead's own ease rate (dungeon.md §2: 2/s) is asserted nowhere by value — only its 600-step asymptote (110 world units) is checked, by "the look-ahead settles 110 world units ahead of the facing". Since 600 steps (10 s) converges to the same asymptote for any positive rate, a wrong `_lookRate` constant would not be caught; the position's two rate criteria ("eases across/down at … per second") have no look-ahead counterpart.
  - No criterion tracks a `facing` change on an already-settled camera (every settled camera in the test helper holds one `Facing` for its whole life), so a bug that snaps `_look` instantly on a facing flip, instead of easing it, is not reachable.
  - "a zone narrower than the view keeps its left edge at the view's left" exercises only the x-axis of `_clamp`/`_goalLeft`; no symmetric criterion exercises a zone shorter than the 750-unit view on the y-axis (`_goalTop`), though the same `_clamp` function is shared.
  - No criterion resizes the viewport after the camera has already `track`ed and `step`ped onto a target (the two resize-only criteria call `resize` before any `track`/`step`), so a live resize's interaction with an already-placed camera is untested.
  - The constraints "`step`, `track`, `confine` and `resize` allocate nothing… `toWorld` and `toScreen` build only the value they return" and "no member beyond the Interfaces table is public" have no automated test (not mechanically checkable by `flutter test`); by reading the file, both hold today — fields are plain `double`/`int`/`bool`/`WorldBox?`, `toWorld`/`toScreen` each allocate exactly the `Vec2`/`ScreenVec2` they return, and the public surface is exactly `viewHeight`, `resize`, `confine`, `track`, `step`, `toWorld`, `toScreen`.
