     criterion as worded — but the `Interfaces` table gives `KeyUp` and `ButtonUp` the same
     value-equality role as `KeyDown`/`ButtonDown`, and nothing in the suite would catch a bug
     planted in `KeyUp.==` or `ButtonUp.==` (e.g. comparing the wrong field, or always returning
     `false`/`true`).
