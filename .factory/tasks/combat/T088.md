# T088 — Test strikes, blocks and reflects through the loop

## Description

Combat tested with the loop (testing.md §7). The test builds, all real:
- a `GameLoop` over an `EventQueue`, stepping `StepEngagements`, `StepFlights` and
  `StepStatusEffects` in that order;
- the in-memory repositories, a `CombatantRoster`, `EngagementQueue`, `BodyStatusEffects` and
  `ReportExchange`.

The bodies are `Combatant` doubles, and `CollisionWorld` is a mock whose `cast` returns null: the
bodies and the world belong to other features, so they are the edges here. The mocks are declared
in the test file, as in `test/integration/ui_kit_test.dart`.

## Preconditions

- On `main`: `EventQueue`, `StepClock`, and `GameLoop({queue, clock, systems})` with
  `advance(Seconds)`.
- On `feat/combat`: T082, T083, T085, T086 and T087.

## Acceptance criteria

- A frame is `loop.advance(Seconds(1 / 60))`.
- A `GameEvent` handler on the queue records `published`.
- The foe is `EntityId(1)`, hurtbox centre `Vec2(0, 0)`, half 20 × 40.
- Akeris is `EntityId(2)`, side `akeris`, hurtbox centre `Vec2(40, 0)`, half 20 × 96.
- Neither is invulnerable or immune unless a test says so.
