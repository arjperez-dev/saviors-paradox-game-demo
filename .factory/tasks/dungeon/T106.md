# T106 — Test a zone from its seed to its collisions through the loop

## Description

Dungeon tested with the loop (testing.md §7). The test builds, all real:
- a `GameLoop` over an `EventQueue`, stepping `StepZone`;
- an `InMemoryZoneRepository`, `SeededZoneGenerator(kits: [SuspendedSanctuaryKit()])`,
  `ZoneCollisionWorld`, `BossRoomDoors` and `ZoneAltars`.

Nothing is mocked: dungeon knows nobody (architecture §2.3).

## Preconditions

- On `main`: `EventQueue`, `StepClock`, and `GameLoop({queue, clock, systems})` with
  `advance(Seconds)`.
- On `feat/dungeon`: T101–T105.

## Acceptance criteria

A frame is `loop.advance(Seconds(1 / 60))`; a `GameEvent` handler on the queue records
`published`.

| Test file | Test name | Assertion |
|---|---|---|
| `test/integration/dungeon_zone_test.dart` | a run's seed becomes a zone the loop publishes | After `generate(ZoneNumber.first, Seed(42))` and one frame, `published` holds exactly one `ZoneEntered` (zone 1, `suspendedSanctuary`), and `stepZone.state` has 36 to 44 rooms, 13 of them on the main path (`04-zones/zone-1-suspended-sanctuary/layout.md` §B.1–§B.2). |
| `test/integration/dungeon_zone_test.dart` | a body dropped at the start lands on the generated floor | `collisionWorld.sweep(WorldBox(centre: state.start - Vec2(0, 200), halfWidth: 20, halfHeight: 96), Vec2(0, 400))` has normal `Vec2(0, -1)` and point y `state.start.y - 96` within 1e-9. |
| `test/integration/dungeon_zone_test.dart` | the drifting platforms move while the loop steps | For the first of seeds 1–10 whose zone holds platforms, 60 frames change at least one of the repository's `load()!.platforms` boxes (`04-zones/zone-1-suspended-sanctuary/hazards.md`). |
| `test/integration/dungeon_zone_test.dart` | opening sealed doors sounds through the loop | After the zone's `sealBossRoom()`, `doors.unseal()` and one frame, `published` holds one `SoundEffectCued` with sound `sfx.bossDoor.open`, and `stepZone.state.isBossRoomSealed` is false (`04-zones/zone-1-suspended-sanctuary/boss-room.md` *Exit*). |
| `test/integration/dungeon_zone_test.dart` | an item returned to an altar shows in the zone's state | `altars.returnTo(state.altars.first.altar, AltarShield(shield: ice))` makes `stepZone.state.altars.first.item` an `AltarShield` with `shield: ice`. |
