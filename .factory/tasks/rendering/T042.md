# T042 — Test the world camera stepping inside the loop

## Description

`FollowCamera` (T040) stepped by the real loop as a world system, as `core/app` will step it: an
`EventQueue`, a `StepClock` and a `GameLoop` whose one world system is the camera's `step`. A halt
of the world holds the camera with everything else; the camera follows again once the halt ends.
Nothing is mocked.

## Preconditions

- `EventQueue`, `StepClock`, `GameLoop` and `SystemStep` of `core/loop` are on `main` (T005–T008,
  T034–T036).
- T040's `FollowCamera` on `feat/rendering`, as its `Interfaces` declare.

## Interfaces

| File | Symbol | Role | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lib/core/rendering/presentation/follow_camera.dart` | `FollowCamera` | uses | `final class FollowCamera implements WorldCamera` |
| `lib/core/loop/application/game_loop.dart` | `GameLoop` | uses | `GameLoop({required EventQueue queue, required StepClock clock, required List<SystemStep> systems, …})` |
| `lib/core/loop/application/event_queue.dart` | `EventQueue` | uses | `final class EventQueue implements EventSink, EventSource` |
| `lib/core/loop/domain/step_clock.dart` | `StepClock` | uses | `StepClock({Seconds maxStep, WorldHalts? halts})` |

## Acceptance criteria

| Test file | Test name | Assertion |
|---|---|---|
| `test/integration/rendering_camera_test.dart` | the camera follows its target while the world steps | With `camera.step` the loop's one world system, after one frame placing it, moving the tracked target 300 world units right and advancing 30 frames of 1/60 s moves `toWorld(ScreenVec2.zero).x` right. |
| `test/integration/rendering_camera_test.dart` | the camera holds still while the map halts the world | After a `MapOpened` is published and delivered, moving the tracked target and advancing 30 frames leaves `toWorld(ScreenVec2.zero)` equal (`==`) to its value before them. |
| `test/integration/rendering_camera_test.dart` | the camera follows again once the map closes | After the halt above, publishing `MapClosed` and advancing 30 frames moves `toWorld(ScreenVec2.zero)` toward the moved target. |
